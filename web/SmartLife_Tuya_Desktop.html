Appearance

Connection

Tuya's cloud API is called straight from your browser. The US and EU endpoints accept direct browser calls; the China endpoint (and some networks) need a CORS proxy in between.

Leave empty for a direct connection. Use {url} in the text if your proxy expects the target address as an encoded query parameter.

Stream gateway & cameras

Cameras you add (✎ Edit → ＋ Add camera) stream over RTSP, which browsers can't play directly. Point ▶ Watch at a small stream gateway on your network — go2rtc or MediaMTX — and the stream plays right in this page. The gateway needs a stream with the same name as the camera here, fed from the same RTSP address (the card's Copy URL button gives you the exact address). Leave the address empty to hand streams to an external player app (such as VLC) instead.

Saved login

No login is saved in this browser.

Hidden devices

Devices and cameras you hid with the ✕ on their card. They stay hidden until you bring them back here.

Help

How login works. This page signs in to Tuya's cloud with the same account you use in the Smart Life (or Tuya Smart) app, then asks Tuya for your device list and sends control commands — the same approach as the classic Smart Life web dashboards. Pick the region where your account was registered (the country you chose when you created the account in the app); the wrong region is the most common cause of “login failed”.

Your credentials. Your username and password go directly to Tuya's servers (px1.tuya*.com) over HTTPS. If you tick “Keep me logged in”, they are kept in this browser's local storage so the app can silently sign back in when the session token expires — use it only on your own devices, and use “Forget saved login” above to erase them.

The proxy. Browsers block some cross-site calls. The US and EU Tuya endpoints allow direct calls from web pages, so no proxy is normally needed there. The China endpoint doesn't, so in “Auto” mode CN traffic is routed through a community CORS proxy (cors.smartathome.co.uk). You can pick a different proxy or your own above — note a proxy can see the requests it forwards (they contain a session token, never your password after sign-in… the sign-in request itself does contain your password, so only use proxies you trust for CN logins).

Customising. Sections sort A–Z by name by default. Use ✎ Edit to drag devices into your own order inside a section (that order then sticks, and each section has a “Reset to name order” button), create groups (rooms), rename devices with an alias, or hide devices you never use. Use the 📌 button on any card to pin a shortcut copy of it in the Pinned section at the top — the device also stays in its group below. Scenes always live in their own Scenes section. Everything is remembered in this browser.

Cameras (RTSP). Tapo cameras: in the Tapo app, open the camera → Settings → Advanced Settings → Camera Account and create a camera account, and make sure RTSP is enabled for the camera. The username and password you add here are that camera account — not your TP-Link cloud account. Then use ✎ Edit → ＋ Add camera and enter the camera's IP address (a fixed or DHCP-reserved IP works best, so the address doesn't change). The app builds the stream address for you: rtsp://user:pass@IP:554/stream1 (high quality) or /stream2 (standard) — or pick “Custom path” for other RTSP cameras. Camera cards sit alongside your devices and can be pinned, grouped and reordered just like them; their passwords are stored in this browser with the same light obfuscation as the saved login.

Watching a stream. Web browsers can't play rtsp:// directly. Without a gateway, ▶ Watch hands the stream address to a player app installed on your device (such as VLC) — or use Copy URL and paste the address into VLC's “Open Network Stream”. For in-browser playback, run a small stream gateway such as go2rtc or MediaMTX on your network, give it a stream with the same name as the camera here (fed from the same RTSP address — Copy URL has it), and enter the gateway's address in Settings → Stream gateway & cameras. ▶ Watch then plays right in this page.

Limits. Tuya rate-limits the device-list call; if you refresh very often the app may briefly show the last known states. Autorefresh every 30s or slower is comfortable.
